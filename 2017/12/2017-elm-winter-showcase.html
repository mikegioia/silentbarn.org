2017 ELM Winter Showcase

Sat, December 16 '17 – Sat, December 16 '17

5:30pm – 8pm

View Facebook Event

It's finally here! We can't wait to celebrate our semester of monster magic with all of you

The 2017 ELM Winter Showcase will feature performances from the Educated Little Monsters Live Band, Hip Hop artists, dance department, and musical theater department, as well as a visual art exhibit! Since our 10-week cycle began in October, we have worked with almost 30 young artists every week, ranging from ages 7 to 20. Some came in as total beginners, and used the open classes to figure out what they wanted to do, and others are veterans, like the older students in the more professional Hip Hop team Monsters Of Brooklyn. You'll see a little bit of both at our 2017 Winter Showcase!

5:30pm Doors Open 6:00-8:00pm Performances

We suggest a $10-$20 donation at the door, and no one will be turned away for lack of funds. $10-$20 ADULTS 18 + $5 TEENS 12-17 KIDS FREE 11 AND UNDER
