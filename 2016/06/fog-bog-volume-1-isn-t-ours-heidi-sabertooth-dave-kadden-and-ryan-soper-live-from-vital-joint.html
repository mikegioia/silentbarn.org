Fog Bog Volume 1: Isn't Ours-Heidi Sabertooth-Dave Kadden and Ryan Soper Live from Vital Joint

Sun, June 26 '16

$88:30pm @ Vital Joint

View Facebook Event

Title:Point is proud to present Fog Bog: Volume 1. A new, misty, psychedelic, experimental music series curated by Ryan William Downey.

Featuring Live Sets by

Isn't Ours Heady, propulsive, ambient, techno from Mike Sheffield of Heaven's Gate. https://soundcloud.com/bilateral-symmetry/isnt-ours-live-pa-alone-together-vol-7

Heidi Sabertooth Brooklyn-based singer, multi-instrumentalist, currently exploring solo and collaborative sample-based avant electronics. https://soundcloud.com/heidisabertooth

Dave Kadden and Ryan Soper A new duo featuring oboe/keyboardist Kadden (Chat Logs, Invisible Circle, Oneida) and modular synth wiz Soper, who recently put out this blistering track with Greg Fox.

http://www.npr.org/event/music/479578783/fox-soper-duo-opens-portals-to-neon-realms-in-i-video https://invisiblecircle.bandcamp.com/

Visuals provided by Wendy Carlos Williams DJ Spineless Chickenshit

VENDORS! While at the show stop by and visit two fantastic vendors who will be joining us:

Nothing Matters NY Pan-African, political, and pop culture embroidery by Dennis Thomas. https://www.instagram.com/nothingmatters_ny/

Paperclip Pottery Mia Schachter makes stuff with her hands out of mud, water, and fire. https://www.instagram.com/paperclippottery/

@ Vital Joint inside The Silent Barn Doors 830 Show 9pm titlepoint.org

*fog and strobe lights will be used in this performance
