Beats & Brunch with Euglossine / Shara Lunon / Opaline / Sunmoonstar

Sun, August 7 '16

1pm

View Facebook Event

some of our favorite days are when we have the chance to introduce you to our friends from around the world who've dedicated themselves to nurturing and encouraging independent arts communities. this sunday we welcome family from gainesville, florida + portland, oregon + sydney, australia into the silent barn for a very special afternoon of beats & brunch. for those who've kept an eye on elestial sound, the church of holy colors, purr tapes, replicant, and so many other projects... come meet the friends you didn't know you have. for everyone else, come make new ones!

♫ euglossine (florida), https://soundcloud.com/euglossine ♫ shara lunon (florida) https://soundcloud.com/sharalunon ♫ opaline (oregon) https://soundcloud.com/crystalhymns ♫ sunmoonstar(australia) https://soundcloud.com/sunmoonstar

bring your own brunch & we'll be making mimosas. great food + coffee nearby: best deli, bookkeepers, bunna, bushwick pita palace, express yourself, jessi's, la lupe, lil mo's, living room, los arcos, mr kiwi's, norbert's, recafo, regalo
