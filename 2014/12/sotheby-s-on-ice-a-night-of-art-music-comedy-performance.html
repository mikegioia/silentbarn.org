Sotheby's on Ice: A Night of Art, Music, Comedy & Performance

Sun, December 7 '14

$77pm

View Facebook Event

Join us for the first ever Sotheby's on Ice, featuring a model collection of artists, musicians and comedians from all around. We'll have performances, videos, music, artwork for display and sale, and all kinds of fun stuff.

featuring...

Art by Mike Abrusci Lorelei Ramirez Max Rosen Dan Licata

Comedy by Brett Davis Jo Firestone Colin Michael Fitzgerald Burgess

Videos by Becca Kauffman Joe Pera

Music by Ryan Conaty Nick Grunerud

curated by Mary Houlihan, Steven DeSiena, Tynan DeLong
