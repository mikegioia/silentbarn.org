Silent Radio

Hosted by Mike Nigro, Silent Radio is both a program review of the Silent Barn's recent events and an auditory extension of the venue’s aesthetic. Equal parts entertaining, informative, and confounding, Silent Radio aims to evoke the free flowing, collaborative atmosphere found at the Silent Barn. Read more about Mike.

Mike Nigro is an active musician and has been a supporter of regional, independent music and art since he was a teenager. He lives in Brooklyn, New York where he runs the record label Oxtail Recordings.
