Paper Jam Small Press Festival

Sat, October 1 '16

12pm – 6:30pm

View Facebook Event

Paper Jam brings together a selection of local up and coming small press artists and cross pollinates their work with the art and music scene of the Silent Barn. Paper Jam is fully curated by: Robin Enrico, G.W. Duncanson, and Ø.K. Fox.

Exhibitors: JMC Aggregate, Jenny Zych, Mike King + Benji Nate, Stephanie Rodriguez, Sara Lautman, Sam Henderson, Darryl Ayo, L. Nichos, Alyssa Berg, Sven Hegedus, Krystal Downs, Troll Hole, La Liga Zine, Annie Mok, Posture Magazine, Sarah Crowe, Morgan Sturgeon, Bjorn Daniel Miner, Cyril Mazard, Daphne Brophy, & Gonzalo Guerrero
