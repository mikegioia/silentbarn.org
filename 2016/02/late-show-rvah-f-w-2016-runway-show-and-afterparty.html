**Late Show** RVAH F/W 2016 Runway Show and Afterparty

Sat, February 13 '16 – Sun, February 14 '16

$1011:59pm – 4am

View Facebook Event

RVAH F/W 2016 "RELEASE/REBIRTH" NYFW RUNWAY SHOW AND AFTERPARTY

11:59 PM - 4:00 AM

SILENT BARN - 603 BUSHWICK AVE, BROOKLYN, NY 11206

RVAH PRESENTS "RELEASE/REBIRTH" F/W 2016

CLEVELAND OHIO NATIVE RVAH PRESENTS THEIR 5TH CONSECUTIVE NYFW RUNWAY SHOW.

THIS SEASON RVAH WILL PRESENT AN ADVANCED TAKE ON UNDERGROUND CULTURE, MUSIC, AND LIFESTYLE.

AFTERPARTY TO BEGIN IMMEDIATELY AFTER RUNWAY SHOW

RUNWAY SOUNDTRACK BY:

NYODENE D (CLEVELAND, CHONDRITIC SOUND) - "Nyodene D is an experimental industrial project that seeks to explore the shedding of the interwoven constructs which define oneself towards a state of greater autonomy"

CHANNEL (ALLOY-X) - Alloy-X label mates Connor Musarra and Ghost Noises have been collaborating for years with frequent appearances on each other's solo releases. In 2014 as they were preparing their second runway soundtrack for RVAH NYFW, they had an excess amount of demos that they had created together, ultimately leading to the birth of Channel. Since their inception, the duo has contributed runway soundtracks for 3 seasons of RVAH NYFW as well as their debut EP, Absolutely Nothing via Alloy-X.

AFTERPARTY LIVE PERFORMANCES BY:

NEGATION (NYC, ASCETIC HOUSE) - After witnessing his stunning performance in Cleveland last June with fellow industrial innovator Shredded Nerve, the RVAH crew is flattered to be able to include NEGATION on the lineup for our afterparty. Combining suffocating industrial dirges with lyrics touching upon themes of surveillance, modern life, and the human body caught between both, NEGATION's debut release on the label of like minded associates ASCETIC HOUSE left a lasting impact on the RVAH crew both for it's stylistic cues and uncompromising political standpoints.Evocative industrial clamours for a new world. https://soundcloud.com/negationnyc

NICK KLEIN (NYC, PRIMITIVE LANGUAGES) - There are many faces to the man known as Nick Klein; whether it be IN TRAINING alumni, PRIMITIVE LANGUAGES label partner, or being voted "the hottest bachelor in techno 2015", the NYC by way of Miami transplant's singular brand of body music sounds like it could be either blaring from a packed club or the dim back room of the club itself. Fresh off a Euro tour supporting his 12" on UNKNOWN PRECEPT and dedicated to the freak electronic curation of the PRIMITIVE LANGUAGES label he runs with MIGUEL ALVARINO, RVAH is proud to present a friend as supportive of Cleveland as Klein and to have everyone dance to the sound of gleaming cowboy boots on the runway. https://soundcloud.com/nick-klein http://unknownprecept.bandcamp.com/album/failed-devotee

CONNOR MUSARRA (NYC, ALLOY-X) - After nearly a decade of releases under the pseudonym Urbindex on labels like Schematic and his own Alloy-X, Cleveland native Connor Musarra has dropped the stage name and is set to debut his new music in this first performance under his real name. Specializing in his unique brand of club focused yet off-kilter electronic music, Musarra has contributed runway soundtracks in collaboration with fellow Alloy-X signee Ghost Noises for 3 seasons of RVAH NY Fashion Week events.

GHOST NOISES (CLEVELAND, ALLOY-X) - Ghost Noises is a darkly psychedelic hip-hop project, intertwining broken samples and dynamic vocals over minimal, bass-heavy production; taking part in a deviant streak steeped in decades of Northeast Ohio music tradition. Their latest release "Zen Archer" is out via Alloy-X Records.

AND DJS: ADAB KIERNAN LaVEAUX RVAH
